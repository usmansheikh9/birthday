Alishba,

The first time I saw you, you were sitting at the canteen with your friends. Hijab, dark red lipstick, that caramel face glowing like it had only ever seen beautiful things. The whole place was full of people and I didn't see a single one of them. Just you.

I got so protective so fast it scared me. I didn't tell my friends. I didn't tell anyone. Everyone in that canteen already looked at you without knowing they were doing it, and I hated it. I wanted you to disappear from everyone's eyes and stay only in front of mine. I was terrified someone would find your name before me, send you a request before me, and I'd lose you before I ever had you.

Then I walked into that canteen every single day looking for you. Months of it. Yearning like an idiot, never saying a word.

When you finally accepted my request I couldn't believe it was real. I had never felt like that in my life. And then I still didn't message you for two whole weeks because I was terrified you'd find me unattractive, terrified you'd be annoyed, because you carried yourself with this dignity that made me want to be careful with you. So instead I dropped hints on my stories. Posted songs. Quietly copied what you posted. Hoping you'd notice without me having to be brave.

I got brave eventually. Best thing I ever did.

Because now, in plain words, you are my home. You're my comfort. The place I run to willingly. The person I could wait hours for and never mind. The one I can spend twenty-four hours with and still not be bored. The person I yawn in front of, sneeze in front of, cough, smell bad, look bad, with zero fear of being judged. You are my whole world.

Happy birthday. I'm so glad I sent that request.

Yours,
Usman Sheikh
