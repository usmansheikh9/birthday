Dear Alishba,

Some people you only ever meet because Allah decided you should. I am sure of that now. A person like you does not just happen. You are the kind of thing the universe makes once and then forgets how.

The day I first saw you I was not looking for anything. It was an ordinary day in the RC and then it was not, because you walked in. Dark red lipstick. That warm caramel skin catching the light. Your hijab, and beneath it a pair of eyes I still have not found words big enough for. I have lived long enough to have seen beautiful people and kind people and clever people. I had never seen you. There is no one else like you and I knew it in the first second.

I told myself it was a crush. I told myself it was infatuation and that it would pass. But my heart started running the moment your time to come to the RC got close, and my mind would go quiet and useless, and a crush does not do that to a person. I knew for certain the day I found myself alone in the mosque for hours, asking Allah for one thing only. To be near you. That is not a crush. That is something written.

And it scared me. There were so many people around you. So many who could speak easily and stand tall and say the things I could not. I was in a race I never agreed to run and I was losing my nerve. For weeks I did not say your name to a single friend. I just carried it. Then I stopped waiting and I went back to Allah, again and again, until one day your name fell into my hands like something I was always meant to hold. I sent the request. You said yes. And everything after that is the best part of my life.

Because now, in the plainest words I have, you are home. You are the place I run to on purpose. The person I would wait hours for and count it as nothing. I could spend a whole day beside you and beg for one more. I can yawn in front of you and sneeze and cough and look my worst and smell my worst and never once feel judged. That is the rarest thing in this world. You made me unafraid.

You are my whole world, Alishba.

Happy birthday. Of everything I have ever done, sending that request is the one I thank Allah for most.

Yours truly,
Usman Sheikh
